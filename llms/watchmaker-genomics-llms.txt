# Watchmaker Genomics
> High-performance NGS library prep and engineered enzymes. Maximize sensitivity and sequencing economy for clinical and translational research

Website: https://www.watchmakergenomics.com

## About
Watchmaker Genomics develops high-performance enzymes, library preparation solutions, and molecular biology reagents that enable more accurate and reliable genomic research and molecular diagnostics. Our technologies are designed for researchers, assay developers, clinical laboratories, and biotechnology organizations.
